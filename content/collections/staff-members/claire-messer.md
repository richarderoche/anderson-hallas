  <p>Claire brings a curiosity for both design and history to her role as an Architectural Intern at Anderson Hallas. With a Master of Architecture from the University of Colorado Denver, she is passionate about historic preservation and creating spaces that highlight the natural beauty of materials and their surroundings. She especially enjoys digging into the history of the built environment and learning how the past can inform thoughtful design today. At AH, Claire is excited to contribute to both new construction and preservation projects across Colorado and throughout the country, particularly through the firm’s work with the National Park Service. An active member of AIA Colorado, WiD, and Design Like a Girl, Claire embraces opportunities to learn, ask questions, and grow as a designer. Outside the office, she enjoys biking, camping, ATVing, and spending time with her family and her dog.
