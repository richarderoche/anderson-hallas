  <p>Kady is a Job Captain who brings a thoughtful, research-driven approach to her work at Anderson Hallas. She enjoys diving into the details and contributing to projects that serve the public while responding carefully to their surrounding environments.
  </p>
  <p>Kady earned her Bachelor of Design with a minor in Sustainability from the University of Florida and went on to complete her Master of Architecture at the University of Michigan. Drawn to architecture by a love of art and design, she values the ability to combine hand drawing with the tangible impact of the built environment.
  </p>
  <p>Kady is actively engaged in the profession through NCARB and thrives in collaborative settings, always eager to learn from her teammates and supporting disciplines. She especially enjoys site visits, where research and observation inform thoughtful design decisions.
  </p>
  <p>Outside of the office, Kady can be found camping, playing tennis, drawing and painting, or experimenting in the kitchen.
  </p>
