  <p>Sharing the adventure of the Grand Canyon, with 5,000,000 visitors per year, the Plaza at the Bright Angel Trailhead offers comfort, shade, seating, restrooms and water stations to hikers and rim strollers. The design challenge required a complimentary yet distinguishable response to Mary Colter's pinwheel cabins at Bright Angel Lodge (1932).
  </p>

  <li>Serves 1000's of day hikers, overnight trackers, mule riders, and rim walkers on any typical summer day</li>
  <li>One of two historically and extensively visited trailheads accessing the South Rim and basin</li>
  <li>Modern materials and detailing reference Mary Coulter's pinwheel cabins</li>
