  <p>In fall of 2021, Anderson Hallas embarked on an 18-month reinvention and transformation of our studio’s new home. It showcases the breadth of our work both historic reuse and contemporary design. The project included:
  </p>
  <ul>
  <li> Adaptive Reuse of a 1940s bungalow</li>
  <li> Design and materials reflective of Golden’s history</li>
  <li>Contrasting materiality of cool steel and warm wood - inside and out</li>
  <li>Interior spaces reflect the changing workplace needs of today</li>
  <li>Flexible workspaces to serve the diverse needs of the team</li>
  <li>Workspaces include traditional dedicated desks, flex “hotel” desks, a treadmill station and outdoor workspaces</li>
  <li>Our model shop highlights our dedication to Craft</li>
  <li>To support our team we have both a “Phone Booth” and employee wellness room</li>
  <li></li>
  <li>Collaborative spaces include the prominent main conference room, “The Garage” and our Zoom Room</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Sustainability Features:
  </p>
  <li> Adaptive Reuse of an existing building in the heart of downtown Golden</li>
  <li> Reuse of an existing building saved embodied energy</li>
  <li> High efficiency, zoned HVAC system</li>
  <li> Energy efficient lighting</li>
  <li> Envelope and insulation upgrades</li>
  <li> Walkable urban core location</li>
  <li> PV panels and an herb garden for employees currently in the works</li>
