  <p>An adaptive reuse of a campus dormitory into a state-of-the art learning environment.   <li>Strategically wove circulation, way-finding windows and breakout nooks throughout</li>
  <li>New classrooms anchor the ends of the building wings </li>
  <li>Building addition provides large conference/meeting room for conversation to a banquet hall</li>
  <li>Unique exterior architectural detailing transforms a bland 1940s dormitory to DU's campus aesthetic</li>
