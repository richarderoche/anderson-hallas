  <p>Private/public partnership between Breckenridge, Summit County, and private founders made this adaptive re-use project possible. The historic schoolhouse originally built in 1909, was transformed into a community centerpiece. The design is suited to its majestic mountain setting by framing the natural features of Breckenridge’s epic mountain slopes. Nearly 500 townsfolk came for the champagne, to toast the newly transformed building, outside, at night, in the dead of winter.
  </p>

  <li>Multi-purpose community hub includes a stunning library, first run movie theater and gathering spaces</li>
  <li>Intergovernmental partnership between the Town of Breckenridge and Summit County</li>
  <li>Former gymnasium transformed into an award-winning library </li>
  <li>Donated to the City</li>
