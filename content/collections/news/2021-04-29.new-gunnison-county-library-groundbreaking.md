The new Library will feature flexible gathering spaces both inside outside, allowing for multiple uses and sizes of groups. Solar and Geothermal technologies contribute to sustainability and stewardship goals of the design, which responds to this uniquely situated setting and strives to provide the library patrons and staff with an immersive experience. Construction is scheduled to be completed in 2022.
