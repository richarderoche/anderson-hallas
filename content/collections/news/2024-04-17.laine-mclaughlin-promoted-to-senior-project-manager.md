  <p>Anderson Hallas Architects is pleased to announce the promotion of Laine McLaughlin to Senior Project Manager, a reflection of her hard work, dedication to design excellence, and team leadership.
  </p>
  <p>Laine McLaughlin, AIA, is an Associate with Anderson Hallas. Since joining the firm in 2017, Laine has been delighted to work on historic structures ranging from National Historic Landmarks to remote cabins. Dedicated to practical sustainability, she seeks to optimize preservation of the past with performance for the future. Laine excels at leading multidisciplinary teams to deliver complex projects on tight schedules and focuses on developing and empowering our internal team. She reviews grants for the State Historic Fund and serves as Secretary for the Colorado Building Enclosure Council.
