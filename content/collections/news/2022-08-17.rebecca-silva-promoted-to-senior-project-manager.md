  <p>Anderson Hallas Architects is pleased to announce the promotion of Rebecca Silva, AIA, to Senior Project Manager.
  </p>
  <p>Rebecca joined Anderson Hallas in 2015 and has been integrally involved in both new design and adaptive reuse projects to much success. With a passion for the environment, Rebecca is chair of the Lakewood Historic Preservation Commission and leads the firm’s internal Sustainability Committee. Her recent work includes rehabilitation of the Hurricane Ridge Visitor Center – a former ski lodge built in 1951 at Olympic National Park – as well as restoration of the 1890 Pitkin County Courthouse in Aspen and design for the new Gunnison County Library slated to open this fall.
