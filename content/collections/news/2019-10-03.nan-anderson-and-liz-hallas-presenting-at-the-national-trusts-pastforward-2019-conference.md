This session will illustrate the heroics and resources required to save our most iconic places in an era of climate change and increasing wildfires. It will also showcase some of the most demanding stone restoration challenges encountered in the field of preservation.<br> <a href="http://www.pastforwardconference.org/">www.PastForwardConference.org</a>
