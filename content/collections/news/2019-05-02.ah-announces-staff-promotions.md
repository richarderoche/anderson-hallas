  <p>Anderson Hallas Architect’s announces the promotions of Brandon Gossard, AIA, to Associate!

He has an undergraduate degree in Geography from the University of Northern Colorado, and a Master’s in Urban Planning and a Master’s in Architecture from the University of Colorado, Denver. Brandon has enjoyed being involved in community engagement projects, from courthouses to libraries, to a recent environmental learning center. Active with the AIA, he also serves as the Secretary of the AIA Colorado Government Affairs Committee.
