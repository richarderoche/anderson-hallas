      <p>By vacating an existing street and placing the new Huerfano
      County Judicial Center at the terminus and in the middle of the vacated road, a
      new civic center campus setting has emerged from the asphalt.
      </p>
      <p>As part of the new building design process, numerous
      studies of alternative sites and different organization of the existing
      historic courthouse and jail building site were conducted. In the end, it was
      determined the best solution with numerous benefits would be to place the new building
      in the middle of the existing street. “While initially a challenge with
      community members who did not want to see the street vacated, this strategy was
      the best overall solution,” stated Bob Kreiman, Courts Executive.
      </p>
      <p>Wells Squier, Principal at Anderson Hallas Architects,
      explained, “it provides several benefits including creating a civic center
      campus setting, and it becomes a one-stop-shop for a variety of government
      business." For the community, it becomes a place for activities and events
      hosted on the campus site. Chief Judge Leslie Gerbracht added, “The outdoor
      spaces will be wonderful for the community. We have always envisioned a place
      for community gathering activities, and now we have that here.”
      </p>
