      <p>Huerfano County is one of Colorado’s most impoverished
      counties. Its socioeconomic indicators consistently show struggling individual
      wealth and economic growth. However, an abundance of nearby natural features
      brings visitors to the area. Walsenburg serves as the gateway to the Great Sand
      Dunes National Park, the Spanish Peaks Wilderness Area, and Colorado’s Scenic
      Highway of Legends. From Walsenburg, recreation enthusiasts will also be able
      to access the planned Colorado Front Range Trail between New Mexico and
      Wyoming. Pass-through traffic from I-25 and US 160 offers an economic stimulus
      to Huerfano County as businesses support travelers with goods and services,
      such as food and beverages, gasoline, lodging, and retail. There are also
      future growth plans for new regional trails, recreational attractions, and
      visitor destinations. When making purchases—such as buying t-shirts—the
      tourist–traveler helps pay for the new Huerfano County Judicial Center with a
      one-cent sales tax. The burden of funding the direly needed courthouse would
      have been seemingly impossible without this sales tax initiative.
      </p>
